06 · Professional development

Reflection & next steps

What changed in my thinking?

Reflect on how your judgment developed, which capabilities you strengthened and what you want to improve next.

### What changed in my thinking? During this project, I began to understand that digital transformation is not simply about introducing new technologies. It requires understanding the company's business model, stakeholders, financial position, workforce, and risks. Comparing KMG with Shell helped me see that successful practices cannot always be transferred directly between companies. They must be adapted to local conditions and regulations. I also improved my ability to analyse business information, evaluate AI applications, work with financial indicators, and turn research into practical recommendations. I learned that AI-generated information must be checked against reliable sources before being used. In the future, I want to strengthen my analytical, strategic, and communication skills. Professional knowledge: Develop a stronger understanding of AI governance, cybersecurity, and digital transformation. 3. Career development: Gain practical experience through internships and prepare for a future career in consulting, particularly at KPMG.
